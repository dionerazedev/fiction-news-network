# Surface brief: The Paw Post (whole site: home, story, category, archive, search, static pages)

Mode: Read. Audience: US phone readers arriving from social shares; job: find a story, read it to the end, share it, find the next one. Constraints: FICTION tag + "This story is fiction." on every story, card and share image; no fake counters, no real people; modern viral-news energy (bold headlines, big photos, mobile-first) is brief-pinned.

Chosen direction: Airmail (recommended on the decision card; user answer pending, building on the recommendation). Degraded roll, no challengers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A news site that arrives like post. The frame borrows the airmail envelope (chevron edge, postmark, route colours) so every story feels hand-delivered; it refuses the white-grid-with-red-accent viral template and the cream broadsheet.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white ground, airmail navy ink (#16234a family) and airmail red (#c8202f family); five stamp-ink route colours, one per category (red, blue, violet, green, ochre). Archivo heavy and semi-condensed for headlines and the wordmark; Source Serif 4 for the reading column. Red/white/navy chevron edge only at the masthead and footer. Circular SVG postmark carries every publish time.

STORY: Readers see today's delivery first, understand at once that it is invented ("FICTION" in the masthead and on every card), read a lead story, and keep following routes (categories) to the next one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chevron edge, then a navy masthead with the wordmark at display size, edition date and the fiction notice; route nav beneath. Below: the lead story's image full width on mobile (left two-thirds on desktop) with a heavy headline, dek and postmark; on desktop the right third lists the rest of today's delivery.

FORM: Airmail envelope and postal wayfinding, position 6 on my ordered list of 7; seed key 4592ea83.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
